OUR STORY

A Note from Our Founder

Every time I shopped online, I noticed the same thing: brands pay commissions to bring customers to their platforms. At the same time, NGOs doing meaningful work spend precious time and effort raising funds.

I began to wonder: what if everyday shopping could help support causes that matter?

That question became Shop4Good.

The idea is simple. You shop with brands you already love through our platform. The brand pays us a commission for referring you, and we share a portion of that commission with an NGO of your choice. You pay nothing extra, and your everyday shopping can help support a cause you care about.

I believe most people want to make a difference, but giving often requires extra time, money, or effort. Shop4Good aims to make supporting good causes a more natural part of everyday life.

We're building Shop4Good around three promises:

Transparency

We'll be clear about how commissions are shared and how they support the causes you choose.

Responsible partnerships

We'll be thoughtful about the NGOs we partner with and the work they do.

Impact that matters

We'll keep the people and communities these causes serve at the heart of what we do.

We're just getting started, and there's a lot to build. Whether you're a shopper, an NGO, or a brand, we'd love for you to be part of this journey.

Thank you for being here.

— The Shop4Good Team

ABOUT SHOP4GOOD

What if something as simple as shopping could help change a life?

Every day, we make choices about what we buy, where we shop, and the brands we support. At Shop4Good, we believe those everyday choices can mean something more.

Shop4Good connects everyday commerce with causes that matter—so that a purchase can become an opportunity, a choice can create hope, and small actions can add up to meaningful change.

We are building a platform that brings consumers, brands, businesses, and credible social-impact organisations together around a simple idea:

“Do good, as you go about your everyday life.”

Everyday shopping → Direct social impact

2% of your purchase directly funds verified causes at zero extra cost to you.

OUR ORIGIN

Why We Started

There are millions of people who want to make a difference. They care about a child's education. They want women to have greater opportunities. They want to contribute to a better society.

Good intentions don't always translate into action.

Life is busy. Giving can feel complicated. And sometimes, we simply don't know where our contribution will make the greatest difference. We wanted to change that.

The hurdle we solve

Purpose made effortless & natural.

Shop4Good is our attempt to make purpose a natural part of everyday life—without asking people to change who they are or how they live.

THE SHOP4GOOD ECOSYSTEM

From Shopping to Impact

Shop4Good brings together products, services, brands, and social-impact initiatives in an ecosystem designed to create shared value.

When you shop through the Shop4Good ecosystem, your participation can help support initiatives run by credible partner organisations.

Our ambition is to make this journey increasingly simple, transparent, and measurable—so you can see that your everyday choices can contribute to something bigger.

Ordinary actions, extraordinary change.

Making a difference doesn't require a grand gesture. It can start with something as ordinary as what you choose to buy.

Privacy Policy

How we manage and protect your personal information at Shop4Good.

Executive Summary

As Shop4Good and therefore custodian of personal information relating to your account, donations, and shopping activity, we must only use that information in accordance with all applicable laws and guidance. This Privacy Notice provides you with a detailed overview of how we will manage your data.

We use your personal information primarily for providing our affiliate shopping platform, processing charitable donations, and improving our services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Information We Collect

  • Identity Data: Name, username, email address.
  • Transaction Data: Details about affiliate clicks, generated donations, and chosen charities.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and platform usage data.

Note: We do not collect or store your payment details or credit card information. All actual purchases happen on our partner marketplaces (e.g., Amazon, Myntra), which have their own privacy policies.

How We Use Your Data

We rely on the following legal justifications to process your data:

  • Contractual: To create your account and allocate generated donations to your chosen cause.
  • Business Need: For quality assurance, platform improvement, and security.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with financial and tax reporting requirements regarding charitable donations.
  • Consent: For marketing communications, where you have explicitly opted in.

Disclosures to Third Parties

We only share necessary data with trusted partners required to operate our service (such as hosting providers, analytics tools, and the charities you support for reporting purposes).

Your Rights

Under data protection law you have certain rights, including:

Access The right to request a copy of your personal data.
Rectification The right to amend inaccurate data.
Erasure The right to delete your account and data.
Portability The right to transfer data to another organisation.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@shop4good.com
